# Published example

[meselson-stahl.ttl](meselson-stahl.ttl) reconstructs an argument from Meselson and Stahl, *The replication of DNA in Escherichia coli* (1958), [original paper](https://dosequis.colorado.edu/Courses/MethodsLogic/papers/Messelson1958.pdf), pp. 676–678, Figures 4–6.

The experiment's labeling observations support conserved parental subunits over retention of the whole parental molecule in one daughter. The example retains the paper's qualification: those results alone did not establish that the subunits were individual polynucleotide chains.

The three Claims link directly to propositions and texts, without duplicate descriptions. The paper's authors are the asserting agents; the example curator records the reconstruction. Two generic ResearchStatements describe competing interpretations without asserting that both are true. The argument and direct discourse links connect the findings to these alternatives.

Preparation derives broader responses and references, and reverses `alternativeTo`. It does not reverse positive or negative responses. `make verify` validates this example with SHACL and includes it in the OWL consistency and semantic checks.
